# Riga Half Marathon — Sub-1:40 Plan

**Race:** Riga Half Marathon, Sun May 16, 2027
**Goal:** sub-1:40 = 7:35/mi (4:43/km) → 1:39:20
**Start point:** Dresden 1:45–1:50 (Oct 25, 2026), then ~4 weeks mostly resting; Achilles tighter after the break.
**Plan:** 25 weeks (Nov 23, 2026 – May 16, 2027) = 11-week rebuild/base lead-in + the **Nike Run Club 14-week Half Marathon plan**, with volume raised to peak at 35 mi/week.

## How the NRC plan was adapted

- **Every NRC speed workout is kept as written** (First Speed Run, Triple 7's, Deuces, 8K Tempo, Speedurance, etc.), with a slightly longer warm-up/cool-down (≈1.5 mi / 1 mi).
- **Volume raised:** NRC recovery runs are extended to 4–6 mi, and long runs are lengthened (NRC's early 5K–10K long runs become 8–12 mi; the 20K becomes 13 mi). Long runs stay NRC-style progression runs.
- **10K tune-up** in NRC week 8 (weekend of Mar 28) replaces that week's Power Pyramid and 15K long run. The Monday after is an easy run instead of NRC wk 7's fartlek.
- **Dress rehearsal** (Apr 17): 8 mi (13.1K) at 7:35/mi, as in NRC.
- **Hills** (Runner Up, Hill Hillier Hilliest): gentle grade; run them flat if the Achilles complains.
- **Race week:** the NRC pre-race speed run moves from Monday to Wednesday.

## Strength and mobility

- **Strength A (gym, Tue):** 90/90 hip switches, trap-bar deadlift, Bulgarian split squat, heavy standing calf raise, seated calf raise, **hip airplanes**, **Copenhagen plank**, pull-up/pulldown, Pallof press.
- **Strength B (home, Thu after the run):** **90/90 hip lift-offs**, single-leg RDL, step-up/split squat, eccentric heel drops (straight + bent knee), single-leg glute bridge, **hip airplanes**, **Copenhagen plank**, monster walks, tibialis raises.
- **Strength C (gym, rebuild Sundays opposite Pilates):** 90/90 switches, leg press, step-ups, heavy calf raises, hip thrust, cable abduction, hip airplanes, Copenhagens, row.
- **Light (taper):** 90/90s, bridges, hip airplanes, short Copenhagens, light calf raises. Last heavy lift is Tue Apr 27; no lifting in race week.
- **Before every run:** Achilles prep — 5×45s wall calf isometric holds + 1 bent-knee hold per side.
- **Daily:** 10-min mobility; hip protocol before speed and long runs.

## Achilles rule

Tightness ≤3/10 that eases as you warm up is fine. Worse the next morning → bike instead of run that day and repeat the previous week's mileage. Still tight by mid-December → see a physio before the base phase.
