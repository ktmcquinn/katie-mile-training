# Katie's Dual-Race Training Plan
### Copenhagen 1-Mile (B-race) → Dresden Half Marathon (A-race)

Built on the **Nike Run Club 14-week Half Marathon** structure, adapted into a two-race plan so you can run a sharp mile on Sep 19 **without** compromising the half on Oct 25.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Today** | Tue, Jun 16, 2026 |
| **B-race** | Copenhagen 1-Mile — **Sat, Sep 19, 2026** · goal sub-6:00 |
| **A-race** | Dresden Half Marathon — **Sun, Oct 25, 2026** · goal sub-1:45 (~8:00/mi) |
| **Weeks to half** | 18.7 |
| **Weeks between races** | 5.1 (mile → half) |
| **Week shape** | 5 runs/wk (2 speed · 2 recovery · 1 long) + your strength & mobility |

---

## How this plan works

The NRC plan is a generic 14-week half plan with no concept of a mid-block mile race. Two things had to be solved:

1. **You're fitter than NRC's starting point.** NRC week 14 opens with a 3-mile long run — a step *backward* from where you are now (~20 mpw). So the NRC **workout types** are kept, but **distances/durations are scaled up** to your current fitness, and the ~5 weeks before the block starts are used as a proper aerobic lead-in rather than wasted.

2. **The mile lands in "6 weeks to go."** Counting back from Oct 25, the mile (Sat Sep 19) falls in NRC's heaviest pre-taper week (stock: hard intervals + 8K tempo + 10-mile long run). Racing a near-maximal mile out of that week would blunt the mile **and** leave you flat. So that week is converted into a **mile race week**: mini-taper in, race, easy recovery out — and the big long run is moved.

### The four phases

| Phase | Dates | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Aerobic Lead-in** | Jun 16 – Jul 19 (~5 wk) | Hold/extend your base, reintroduce light speed & strides, protect the ankle/hip/shin. Sets up the block. |
| **B1 — Base + Strength-Speed** | Jul 20 – Sep 6 ("14"→"8" to go) | NRC speed backbone; long runs build to ~12 mi; mile-specific speed sharpened in the last 2–3 weeks. |
| **B2 — Mile Peak & Recover** | Sep 7 – Sep 27 ("7"→"5" to go) | Mini-taper into the **mile (Sep 19)**, race, then 4–5 easy days to absorb it and pivot back to the half. |
| **B3 — Half Build & Taper** | Sep 28 – Oct 25 ("4"→"1" to go) | Biggest half-specific long runs (peak ~13 mi) + half-pace tempo, then a clean 2-week taper to **race day**. |

## Pace & effort guide

Treat every pace as the *middle of a range* — effort wins on hot/tired days. Paces below are anchored to your goals (sub-6 mile, sub-1:45 half). Find Audio Guided Runs for each session in the NRC app.

| Run type | Effort (/10) | Approx pace |
|---|---|---|
| Recovery / easy | 4–5 | 9:00 – 9:45 /mi |
| Long run (progression) | 5–6, finish faster | start ~9:15 → finish ~8:15 /mi |
| Tempo | 6 | 7:20 – 7:40 /mi |
| 10K pace | 6–7 | 6:55 – 7:10 /mi |
| 5K pace | 7–8 | 6:30 – 6:45 /mi |
| Mile / "best" pace | 9 | 5:55 – 6:05 /mi |
| Half-marathon goal pace | race | ~7:55 – 8:00 /mi |

> **Coach's note:** a genuine sub-6 mile usually implies a half *faster* than 1:45. Don't lock 8:00/mi in stone — after the lead-in, run a 5K time trial (or use the mile result) to recalibrate. You may find 7:40–7:50/mi is the right half target.

## Weekly day template

A repeating rhythm that keeps hard days apart (NRC: never speed on back-to-back days) and easy days truly easy.

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| **Mon** | Rest + 10-min hip/ankle mobility |
| **Tue** | **Speed Run #1** + strength after |
| **Wed** | Recovery run (easy) + mobility |
| **Thu** | **Speed Run #2 / Tempo** + strength after |
| **Fri** | Rest + mobility |
| **Sat** | Recovery run (easy) |
| **Sun** | **Long run** (progression) |

Strength sits *after* the two speed days so your easy/rest days stay genuinely restful. Keep your existing 2×/week strength and daily hip protocol — they're your main insurance against the ankle/hip/shin history. **Mile race week is the one exception** to this template (see below).

## Phase A — Aerobic Lead-in (Jun 16 – Jul 19)

Goal: arrive at Jul 20 healthy and at ~22–24 mpw, with legs that remember how to turn over. Nothing maximal.

| Week | Dates | Long run | Speed touch | ~Volume |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Jun 16 – Jun 21 | 7 mi easy | 4–6 × 20s strides after an easy run | ~20 mi |
| L2 | Jun 22 – Jun 28 | 8 mi easy | Fartlek 6 × (1:00 hard / 2:00 easy) | ~22 mi |
| L3 | Jun 29 – Jul 5 | 8 mi easy | Hill strides 8 × 12s + 6 strides | ~22 mi |
| L4 | Jul 6 – Jul 12 | 9 mi easy | Fartlek 8 × (1:00 hard / 1:30 easy) | ~24 mi |
| L5 | Jul 13 – Jul 19 | 8 mi (cutback) | Light intervals 6 × 1:00 @ 5K, jog 1:00 | ~22 mi |

Otherwise: 2 recovery runs/week, 2 rest days, strength 2×, daily mobility. If anything flares, swap a run for the bike — the lead-in is the cheapest place to be conservative.

## Phase B — The 14-week block (Jul 20 → Oct 25)

Each week keeps NRC's 2 speed + 2 recovery + 1 long. Speed sessions use the NRC Audio Guided Run names so you can pull them up directly. Long runs are scaled to your fitness and the dual-race arc.

### B1 — Base + Strength-Speed

**"14 to go" · Jul 20 – Jul 26 · ~24 mi**
- Speed 1 — *First Speed Run* (Intervals): 5:00 WU, 8 × 1:00 @ 5K, 1:00 jog between.
- Speed 2 — *One Hard Two Easy* (Fartlek): 5:00 WU, 21:00 alternating 1:00 hard / 2:00 easy.
- Recovery ×2 (25–35 min). **Long: 8 mi** progression.

**"13 to go" · Jul 27 – Aug 2 · ~26 mi**
- Speed 1 — *No Time Go Time* (Intervals): 5:00 WU, 1:00 mile / 2:00 5K / 3:00 10K / 2:00 5K / 1:00 mile, 1:00 jog between.
- Speed 2 — *Run Strong. Repeat.*: 5:00 WU, 4 × 1:30 @ 5K, 1 × 1:30 @ mile (×2 sets); 45s after 5K reps, 1:00 after mile.
- Recovery ×2 (30–35 min). **Long: 9 mi.**

**"12 to go" · Aug 3 – Aug 9 · ~27 mi**
- Speed 1 — *Runner Up* (Hills): 5:00 WU, 5 × [45s @ 10K effort + 15s best effort], 1:15/45s recovery.
- Speed 2 — *Triple 7's*: 5:00 WU, 3 × 7:00 @ 5K, 2:30 jog between.
- Recovery ×2. **Long: 10 mi.**

**"11 to go" · Aug 10 – Aug 16 · ~29 mi**
- Speed 1 — *The Rundown* (mile/5K ladder): 5:00 WU, 3×1:00 mile + 3×2:00 5K, 2×1:00 mile + 2×2:00 5K, 1×1:00 mile + 1×2:00 5K.
- Speed 2 — *Tempo Run with Emily Infeld*: 7:00 WU, 25:00 tempo.
- Recovery ×2. **Long: 11 mi.**

**"10 to go" · Aug 17 – Aug 23 · ~31 mi** *(biggest long run before the mile)*
- Speed 1 — *Sneaky Speed*: 5:00 WU, [1×1:30 5K + 3×45s mile] × 3, 1:00 jog between.
- Speed 2 — *Out Strong Back Fast*: 5:00 WU, 23:00 progression tempo.
- Recovery ×2. **Long: 12 mi.**

**"9 to go" · Aug 24 – Aug 30 · ~26 mi (cutback)** — *mile sharpening begins*
- Speed 1 — *Run Fast. Repeat.*: 5:00 WU, 20 × 0:30 @ mile pace (#1 & #11 at 5K), 1:00 jog between. **Pure mile turnover.**
- Speed 2 — *Hill Hillier Hilliest*: 5:00 WU, [1:00 10K + 0:45 5K + 0:30 mile] × 3.
- Recovery ×2. **Long: 10 mi** (cutback to keep legs fresh for speed).

**"8 to go" · Aug 31 – Sep 6 · ~30 mi** — *last big aerobic week before the mile*
- Speed 1 — *The Shifter*: 5:00 WU, descending blocks into 1:00 mile-pace surges (see NRC).
- Speed 2 — *Power Pyramid*: 5:00 WU, 1:00 mile / 5:00 5K / 10:00 10K / 5:00 5K / 1:00 mile.
- Recovery ×2. **Long: 12 mi** (final long run before mile taper).

### B2 — Mile Peak & Recover

**"7 to go" · Sep 7 – Sep 13 · ~23 mi** — *sharpen, start shedding fatigue*
- Speed 1 — *Deuces*: 5:00 WU, 10 × 2:00 @ 5K (2:00 recovery after #4 & #8, else 1:00). Trim to **6–7 reps** if legs feel heavy — freshness > volume now.
- Speed 2 — *One Hard. One Easy.* (Fartlek): 5:00 WU, 15:00 alternating 1:00 hard / 1:00 easy. Keep it crisp, not crushing.
- Recovery ×2 (easy). **Long: 8 mi** (shortened — banking freshness).

**"6 to go" · Sep 14 – Sep 20 · MILE RACE WEEK · ~15 mi** 🏁
> This week **breaks the normal template**. The whole point is to arrive at Saturday's start line fast and fresh.

| Day | Session |
|---|---|
| Mon Sep 14 | Rest + mobility |
| Tue Sep 15 | **Sharpener:** 10:00 easy WU, 6 × 20s @ mile pace w/ full recovery, 4 strides, 10:00 easy. Short and snappy. |
| Wed Sep 16 | Easy 25–30 min recovery + mobility |
| Thu Sep 17 | Rest (or 12-min shakeout + 3 strides) |
| Fri Sep 18 | Full rest or 10-min shakeout + 3 strides; legs up, hydrate |
| **Sat Sep 19** | **🏁 COPENHAGEN MILE.** Thorough warmup: 15 min easy + drills + 4–6 build-up strides to mile pace. Race. 10–15 min easy cooldown. |
| Sun Sep 20 | Very easy 3–4 mi recovery jog (or full rest if sore) |

No long run this week — it's moved to next week. No strength Thu–Sat.

**"5 to go" · Sep 21 – Sep 27 · ~25 mi** — *absorb the mile, pivot to the half*
- Mon–Tue: easy recovery runs only (the mile is a real stressor — respect it).
- Speed 1 (mid-week, once legs are back) — *90's*: 5:00 WU, [1:30 5K / 1:30 10K / 1:30 mile] × 3, 1:30 jog between.
- Speed 2 — *Speedurance*: 7:00 WU, 3×2:00 5K, 10:00 tempo, 3×2:00 5K.
- **Long: 10 mi** (rebuild — first real long run after the mile).

### B3 — Half Build & Taper

**"4 to go" · Sep 28 – Oct 4 · ~31 mi (peak week)**
- Speed 1 — *5 × 5:00 @ 10K Pace*: 5:00 WU, 5 × 5:00 @ 10K, 2:00 jog between.
- Speed 2 — *Two Hard. One Easy.* (Fartlek): 5:00 WU, 21:00 alternating 2:00 hard / 1:00 easy.
- Recovery ×2. **Long: 13 mi** — peak long run; run the **last 3–4 mi at half goal pace (~8:00)**.

**"3 to go" · Oct 5 – Oct 11 · ~28 mi**
- Speed 1 — *Long and Strong and Fast*: 5:00 WU, [8:00 10K / 4:00 5K / 2:00 mile] × 3.
- Speed 2 — *Bring It Down* (15:00 progression tempo): 5:00 WU, 5:00 recovery → 4:00 10K → 3:00 5K → 2:00 mile → 1:00 best.
- Recovery ×2. **Long: 12 mi** with **5–6 mi at half goal pace**.

**"2 to go" · Oct 12 – Oct 18 · ~21 mi (taper begins)**
- Speed 1 — *Stronger Faster*: 5:00 WU, [3:00 5K + 4×0:30 mile] × 3. Sharpness, low volume.
- Speed 2 — *In Control*: 6:00 WU, 1:00 mile / 3:00 5K / 5:00 10K / 7:00 recovery. Keep it controlled.
- Recovery ×2 (short). **Long: 8 mi** easy.

**"1 to go" · Oct 19 – Oct 25 · RACE WEEK · ~20 mi incl. race**
- Mon: rest + mobility.
- Tue — *The Speed Run Before…*: 5:00 WU, 1:00 5K / 2:00 10K / 5:00 @ **half goal pace** / 2:00 10K / 1:00 5K, 1:00 jog between. Primes race rhythm.
- Wed: easy 25 min.
- Thu: easy 20 min + 4 strides.
- Fri: rest.
- Sat: 10–15 min shakeout + 3 strides; off your feet, hydrate, carb-load.
- **Sun Oct 25 — 🏁 DRESDEN HALF MARATHON (13.1 mi).** Even or slightly negative split; settle into ~8:00/mi (or your recalibrated target) by mile 2.

## Coaching notes

**Protecting the half while chasing the mile.** The mile costs you almost nothing in half fitness — it *is* high-end speed work — as long as you respect the 4–5 easy days after it. The plan banks your two longest pre-mile runs (12 mi at "10" and "8" to go), dips for the race, then rebuilds to a 13-mi peak with two half-pace long runs in the final fortnight. That's plenty of specific endurance for a strong half.

**Injury & burnout guardrails (your ankle/hip/shin history).**
- Every easy day stays easy — if a "recovery" run creeps under 9:00/mi, you're doing it wrong.
- Keep the daily 10-min hip/ankle mobility and 2×/week strength all the way through; only drop strength in race weeks (mile week and half week).
- The cutback at "9 to go" and the taper weeks are non-negotiable rest — don't add mileage because you feel good.
- Two consecutive days of unusual soreness, niggle that changes your gait, or resting HR up 7+ bpm → swap a run for the bike or take the day. "Sometimes the best run is no run."

**Fueling.** This pairs with your in-app Fuel targets. Don't under-fuel the build weeks ("10," "8," "4" to go are your highest loads) — energy availability matters most exactly when volume peaks. Practice race-morning fueling on your peak long runs (13/12 mi) so nothing's a surprise on Oct 25.

**Autoregulation.** This is written in pencil. If life or weather compresses a week, protect the **long run** and **one** speed session; the rest is flexible. Recalibrate paces off a 5K time trial or the mile result rather than guessing.
